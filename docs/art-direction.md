# ふわふわの森の素材

ユーザーの「もっと子供が可愛すぎて喜ぶ作りに」という依頼で、既存の絵文字キャラクターをオリジナルのぬいぐるみに置き換えた。

- `assets/rabbit.webp`、`assets/bear.webp`、`assets/cat.webp`
- 内蔵 image_gen で生成。外部の写真・既存キャラクター・子どもの画像は使っていない。
- 各画像は左から笑顔・びっくり・おやすみの3コマ。透明背景を保ってWebPに圧縮。CSSでコマを切り替える。
- 森の小道具はこのリポジトリで描いたSVG。音や点滅は加えず、閉眼達成後は静止する。OSの「視差効果を減らす」にも対応。

## 最終プロンプト（内蔵ツール）

### rabbit

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: a production game sprite strip for a Japanese toddler hide-and-seek web game. Make a wide 3:1 image on a genuinely transparent background, containing exactly THREE equal square panels with no visible panel borders. Each panel has the SAME one extremely adorable original handmade plush animal, same proportions, scale and centered baseline, complete body, comfortably inside its own third without crossing edges. Soft matte felt/wool, subtle fuzzy fibers, mochi-round oversized head, very short chubby arms and feet, tiny chocolate-brown bead eyes, pink rosy cheeks, tiny embroidered mouth. Tasteful premium children's picture book art, warm soft diffuse lighting, simple readable silhouette, no dark outlines. The three poses left to right: (1) delighted welcoming smile with one little paw raised, eyes open; (2) playful surprised 'did you peek?' face, both little paws touching cheeks, eyes open, little round mouth; (3) peacefully asleep sitting, eyes closed in little arcs, paws together, gentle smile. NO accessories floating around, NO props, NO scenery, NO ground, NO opaque background, NO text, NO letters, NO watermark. The body is the same size and position in all three panels. These are charming soft toys, not realistic animals, not existing licensed characters. Subject: a warm ivory bunny with long rounded upright ears, pale peach inner ears, tiny cotton tail, wearing a small sage-green neckerchief. Its ears fit entirely in each square panel. Exceptionally cute, soft and huggable.
